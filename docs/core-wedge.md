# Core Wedge: EU AI Act Readiness Proof Pack

## The Wedge

Zenvyra's core wedge is the **EU AI Act Readiness Proof Pack** for AI startups that sell to enterprise customers.

Enterprise buyers, security reviewers, and procurement teams increasingly ask AI vendors:

- What AI systems do you operate?
- What risk category do they fall under under the EU AI Act?
- What documentation can you share?
- How do you handle transparency, human oversight, and evidence retention?

Most AI startups do not have this documentation ready. Zenvyra gives them a fast, structured way to produce it.

## Who It Is For

- AI-native startups building on LLMs, agents, or ML models
- Teams selling into regulated industries or enterprises with diligence questionnaires
- Founders who need to show operational readiness evidence without hiring a full compliance team first

## What the Proof Pack Includes

A complete proof pack produced by Zenvyra contains:

1. **AI System Inventory**
   - System name, provider, and model details
   - Intended purpose and deployment context
   - EU exposure and affected user groups
   - Human oversight owner and review dates

2. **Risk Classification Rationale**
   - Prohibited, high-risk, limited-risk, or minimal-risk indicator mapping
   - Confidence explanation and classification rationale
   - Clear note that final legal classification requires counsel review

3. **Obligation Mapping**
   - AI Act obligations triggered by the system's classification
   - Evidence types required: policy, model card, risk assessment, log sample, process document, owner attestation, URL

4. **Evidence Gap Table**
   - Current status per evidence item: missing, requested, uploaded, reviewed, approved, stale
   - Owner and due date per gap
   - Next action for the team

5. **Exportable Documents**
   - System card
   - Transparency notice
   - Assessment summary
   - Evidence checklist
   - Optional public verification page

6. **Legal Disclaimer**
   - Every proof pack includes a statement that Zenvyra produces operational readiness evidence, not legal advice or a regulatory compliance guarantee.

## Why This Is the Right Wedge

- **Timely**: The EU AI Act is in force and enterprise buyers are beginning to ask for evidence.
- **Focused**: It narrows the product to a concrete, deliverable artifact instead of a broad compliance platform.
- **Trust-building**: A proof pack helps startups answer diligence questions transparently.
- **Expandable**: The same inventory and evidence model can later support GDPR, CCPA, and other frameworks.

## What Success Looks Like

A startup can sign up, add one AI system, receive a risk classification with rationale, see its evidence gaps, upload or record evidence, and export a proof pack to share with a customer or counsel within a single session.
