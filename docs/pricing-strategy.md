# Zenvyra Pricing Strategy

## Overview
Zenvyra targets businesses that need automated compliance monitoring. Our pricing balances accessibility with enterprise value.

## Target Market
- **SMBs (Small-Medium Businesses)**: 50-500 employees, need basic compliance
- **Enterprises**: 500+ employees, need advanced features and integrations
- **Agencies**: Manage compliance for multiple clients

## Pricing Tiers

### 1. Free Tier
**Price**: $0/month
**Target**: Individual users, small businesses testing compliance
**Features**:
- 5 website scans/month
- Basic compliance reports
- Email alerts
- Community support

**Goal**: Convert 20% to paid plans

### 2. Starter Plan
**Price**: $29/month ($290/year - 20% discount)
**Target**: Small businesses, startups
**Features**:
- 50 website scans/month
- Advanced AI analysis
- PDF reports
- Email + Slack alerts
- Basic API access
- Email support

**Goal**: Main conversion tier

### 3. Professional Plan
**Price**: $99/month ($990/year - 20% discount)
**Target**: Growing businesses, agencies
**Features**:
- 500 website scans/month
- Multi-language support
- Competitor analysis
- Team collaboration (up to 10 users)
- Advanced integrations (Zapier, API)
- White-label reports
- Priority support
- Custom policies

**Goal**: High-value conversions

### 4. Enterprise Plan
**Price**: $299/month ($2990/year - 20% discount)
**Target**: Large enterprises, regulated industries
**Features**:
- Unlimited scans
- Advanced monitoring (real-time)
- SSO integration
- Custom integrations
- Dedicated account manager
- SLA guarantees
- On-premise deployment option
- Phone + email support

**Goal**: Enterprise contracts

## Pricing Strategy Principles

### 1. Value-Based Pricing
- Price based on value delivered, not just features
- Focus on ROI: "Save X hours of compliance work"

### 2. Freemium Funnel
- Free tier → Starter → Professional → Enterprise
- Clear upgrade triggers at usage limits

### 3. Annual Discounts
- 20% discount for annual billing
- Encourages commitment and reduces churn

### 4. Feature Gating
- Essential features in lower tiers
- Advanced features (API, integrations) in higher tiers

## Risks & Mitigations

### Risk: Low conversion from free tier
**Mitigation**: Improve onboarding, add more value in free tier, optimize upgrade prompts

### Risk: High churn
**Mitigation**: Focus on customer success, regular check-ins, feature requests

### Risk: Competition
**Mitigation**: Differentiate with AI, build community, focus on SMB market

### Risk: Regulatory changes
**Mitigation**: Stay updated on compliance laws, agile product development
