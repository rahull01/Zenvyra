# Pricing FAQ

Answers to the most common pricing and billing questions. This FAQ is
a source of truth — if you find an answer here that differs from
marketing copy, the FAQ wins. Marketing copy may be rephrased for
clarity but must not contradict the FAQ.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I change plans at any time?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and we prorate the remaining
time on your current plan. Downgrades take effect at the end of the
current billing period so you keep what you paid for.

### Do you offer a free trial?

The Free plan is the trial — you can use Zenvyra on one site forever
at no cost. We do not offer time-limited trials of paid plans at this
time, but the Founder Setup package includes hands-on onboarding for
$199 one-time.

### What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards via Dodo Payments. For the Agency
plan, we can issue invoices for annual contracts (contact
billing@zenvyra.com).

### Can I get a refund?

We do not offer refunds for partial billing periods. If you cancel
mid-cycle, your access continues until the end of the period. If you
believe you were charged in error, contact billing@zenvyra.com within
30 days of the charge.

### Are there usage limits?

Yes. Each plan has per-period limits on the number of websites,
policies, and scans. Limits are soft caps — we'll email you before
they're reached. If you need higher limits than the Agency plan,
contact sales for custom pricing.

### Can I downgrade?

Yes. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period.
After downgrade, your data and history are preserved; the only effect
is which features and limits are active.

### What's included in the Founder Setup package?

The Founder Setup is a $199 one-time package that includes:

- Draft policies (AI usage, privacy, cookie, terms, refund, shipping)
- Cookie banner and consent preferences configured
- Initial AI readiness and privacy proof PDF report
- Privacy-safe public trust certificate
- Platform-specific fix instructions (Shopify, WordPress, Webflow,
  custom code)

It is operational implementation support, not legal advice.

### Is this legal advice?

No. Zenvyra is readiness automation software, not a law firm. We help
teams build and maintain EU AI Act readiness evidence, but the legal
interpretation and any regulatory filings remain your responsibility
(or your counsel's). See `docs/legal-disclaimer.md` for the full
disclaimer.

### Can I use Zenvyra if I'm outside the EU?

Yes. The EU AI Act applies to providers and deployers that place AI
systems on the EU market or affect EU users. Even if you are based
elsewhere, Zenvyra's readiness evidence is useful for enterprise
customers and procurement teams that ask about AI Act alignment.

### Do you offer SSO / SAML?

SSO (SAML 2.0 and OIDC) is on the roadmap. Today, Agency customers can
use Google OAuth. Enterprise SSO is available for custom contracts;
contact sales.

### What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your data is retained for 90 days after cancellation, then permanently
deleted. Within the 90-day window you can re-subscribe and regain
access without losing anything.

### Do you offer a discount for nonprofits or open-source projects?

Yes. Reach out to support@zenvyra.com with a brief description of your
project and we'll evaluate.

### How does the Agency plan differ from Pro?

Agency adds:

- Up to 50 websites (vs. 10 on Pro)
- White-label proof packs and certificates
- Higher scan quotas
- Dedicated support channel
- White-label branding for client deliverables

If you need unlimited websites or custom branding, contact sales.

### Can I share my account with a teammate?

Yes — invite teammates from Dashboard > Team. Roles:

- **OWNER** — full control
- **ADMIN** — manage members and edit the organization
- **MEMBER** — use the workspace
- **VIEWER** — read-only

### Is there a per-seat fee?

No — plans are priced by capacity (sites, scans, policies), not by
seat. Invite unlimited teammates.

### What if I need something not listed here?

Email support@zenvyra.com — we read every message.
