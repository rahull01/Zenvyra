# Design-Partner Onboarding Flow

## Goal

Convert 5–10 ideal first customers into active design partners who help validate the EU AI Act Readiness Proof Pack workflow in exchange for discounted or free access, direct support, and input into the roadmap.

## Partner Criteria

A strong design partner matches the ideal first customer profile and has:

- At least one active enterprise deal or pilot in progress
- Willingness to spend 30–60 minutes per week giving feedback
- A technical founder or product lead who can execute onboarding steps
- Permission to share anonymized findings for case studies

## Onboarding Flow

### Week 1: Qualify and Align

1. **Application or Outreach**
   - Collect: company name, stage, AI systems in use, target enterprise buyers, biggest diligence pain point
   - Confirm fit against the ideal customer profile

2. **Kickoff Call (30 min)**
   - Understand current AI Act readiness process
   - Identify the first enterprise objection they need to answer
   - Set expectations: weekly check-in, feedback via Slack/email, case study option

3. **Account Setup**
   - Create design-partner account with Pro-tier access
   - Apply founder-led onboarding label for direct support

### Week 2: First Inventory

1. **Guided System Entry**
   - Partner adds 1–3 AI systems to the inventory
   - Review intended purpose, provider, EU exposure, and user-facing indicators together

2. **Risk Classification Review**
   - Walk through Zenvyra's classification rationale
   - Compare against partner's own legal assessment
   - Capture where the rationale is wrong, unclear, or missing context

3. **Gap Discussion**
   - Review evidence gaps
   - Prioritize 2–3 gaps the partner can close quickly

### Week 3: Evidence and Export

1. **Evidence Upload**
   - Partner uploads or records evidence for prioritized gaps
   - Review evidence status workflow: missing → uploaded → reviewed → approved

2. **Proof-Pack Export**
   - Generate system card, transparency notice, assessment summary, and checklist
   - Partner shares the proof pack with their prospect or counsel
   - Collect feedback on format, completeness, and tone

3. **Public Verification Page (Optional)**
   - If appropriate, publish a scoped verification page
   - Review what information should and should not be public

### Week 4: Iterate and Expand

1. **Feedback Review**
   - What worked?
   - What was missing?
   - What did the buyer/counsel ask that Zenvyra did not cover?

2. **Roadmap Input**
   - Vote on next features
   - Confirm whether the partner would pay at launch pricing

3. **Case Study Agreement**
   - Document anonymized or attributed success story
   - Schedule launch-day support

## Support Rhythm

- **Daily**: Async Slack/email for blockers during onboarding week
- **Weekly**: 15-minute sync for first month
- **Monthly**: Office hours for all design partners

## Success Metrics

- 100% of partners complete a first AI system inventory
- 80% generate and share a proof pack with a prospect or counsel
- Each partner provides at least 3 pieces of actionable product feedback
- At least 50% of partners confirm willingness to pay at launch pricing

## Incentives

- Free Pro access during the design-partner period
- Founder-led onboarding and direct support
- Input on roadmap prioritization
- Co-branded case study or testimonial opportunity
- Lifetime founder pricing after launch
