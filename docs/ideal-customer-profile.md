# Ideal First Customer Profile

## Primary Profile: AI Startup Selling to Enterprise

### Company Characteristics

- **Stage**: Seed to Series A
- **Size**: 5–50 employees
- **Funding**: Recently raised or revenue-generating
- **Go-to-market**: B2B SaaS, selling to mid-market or enterprise customers
- **Jurisdiction**: Headquartered anywhere, but selling into the EU or planning to

### Product Profile

- Builds on top of OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, or open-source LLMs
- Ships AI-assisted features, agents, or copilots
- Processes user data, generates content, or makes recommendations
- Has at least one feature that could be interpreted as user-facing AI

### Triggering Moment

The ideal first customer has recently experienced one of these:

- An enterprise prospect sent a security or AI governance questionnaire
- A customer asked for AI Act documentation or transparency notices
- Legal counsel flagged AI risk before a major deal closed
- The team realized they cannot describe all AI systems in their product in one document

### Decision-Maker Profile

- **Role**: Founder/CEO, CTO, or Head of Product
- **Age**: 25–40
- **Background**: Technical founder or operator, not a compliance specialist
- **Pain awareness**: High — they have already felt the pain of manual diligence
- **Budget authority**: Can approve $49–$199/month tools without a long procurement cycle

### Jobs to Be Done

1. Quickly inventory AI systems and providers
2. Understand which EU AI Act risk category applies
3. Generate a defensible rationale without hiring a lawyer first
4. Produce evidence documents to share with prospects and counsel
5. Track gaps and next actions as the product evolves

### Why They Buy First

- The problem is urgent and tied to revenue
- The alternative is slow and expensive legal review
- The proof pack format maps directly to buyer questions
- Low price point fits a startup budget

### Why They Might Not Buy

- No enterprise deals in the pipeline
- Believes they are exempt from AI Act scope
- Already has legal counsel handling everything manually
- Prefers to wait until enforcement pressure is visible

### Where to Find Them

- Product Hunt and Hacker News launch threads
- LinkedIn communities for AI founders
- Twitter/X AI startup circles
- Slack/Discord communities for LLM builders
- Y Combinator and other startup alumni networks

### Early Adopter Signal

A team that can articulate the specific enterprise objection they need to overcome — for example, "Our buyer asked whether our recommendation engine is high-risk under the EU AI Act."
