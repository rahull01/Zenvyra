# Agency Setup Guide

Last reviewed: 2026-06-07

This guide explains how UK/US agencies use Zenvyra as white-label privacy compliance proof infrastructure across client websites. The goal is to help agencies sell, install, monitor, and report privacy proof workflows without manually checking every client database record.

Zenvyra provides operational privacy documentation and monitoring evidence. It does not replace legal advice from qualified counsel.

## Agency Positioning

Recommended agency offer:

> Add privacy compliance monitoring to every client website in 48 hours with white-label reports, client trust badges, and monthly proof packs.

Use this positioning for UK/US SMBs, ecommerce brands, Webflow agencies, WordPress agencies, Shopify agencies, and growth marketing agencies.

Do not claim guaranteed compliance. Use language like "privacy readiness," "proof workflow," "monitoring evidence," "policy drafts," and "review packet."

## Workspace Setup

Create the agency workspace before adding client sites:

1. Create the agency account.
2. Assign the owner and implementation users.
3. Configure white-label name, support email, logo, and report footer.
4. Configure billing plan and client-site allowance.
5. Add default monthly proof pack recipients.
6. Add internal escalation contacts for DSAR, payment, technical, and legal review issues.

## Client Site Onboarding

For each client site:

1. Add the client domain.
2. Select platform: WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, GTM, custom site, or other.
3. Set target region: UK, US, or UK+US.
4. Record technical owner and business owner.
5. Run the first scan.
6. Review trackers, policies, forms, DSAR/consumer request coverage, and certificate readiness.
7. Generate the install packet.
8. Install the banner and footer links.
9. Rerun the scan after deployment.
10. Send the first proof pack.

## Required Client Inputs

Collect these before implementation:

- Website admin access or implementation contact.
- Current privacy policy and cookie policy links.
- List of marketing/analytics tools.
- Whether the site targets UK, US, or both.
- Whether the client handles sensitive data categories.
- Support email for privacy requests.
- Approved public company name for certificate display.
- Approval for agency white-label branding.

## White-label Rules

Use white-label branding carefully:

- Public certificate should identify the client site and review status.
- Agency branding can appear as the operator where the client approves it.
- Internal scan notes, emails, billing state, and private remediation comments must not appear publicly.
- Public pages should not claim legal certification or guaranteed compliance.

## Monthly Proof Pack Workflow

Each month, produce a concise proof pack:

- Current privacy readiness score.
- Tracker changes since last scan.
- Consent evidence summary.
- DSAR or consumer request status summary.
- Policy version changes.
- Certificate status.
- Open remediation items with owner and priority.
- "Not legal advice" disclaimer.

For agency retention, deliver the proof pack as an account-management artifact, not only a dashboard screen.

## Escalation Rules

Escalate to the client or counsel when:

- Sensitive data processing appears but is not disclosed.
- New ad pixels, session replay, or data enrichment vendors appear.
- DSAR or consumer request deadlines are at risk.
- The client asks whether a specific practice is lawful.
- A public certificate would misrepresent the site state.
- A payment, webhook, or email failure prevents customer notifications.

## UK/US Review Checklist

For UK sites:

- Cookie and similar technology disclosures are reviewed against PECR expectations.
- Non-essential trackers are controlled where prior consent is required.
- Privacy request workflow is visible.
- Policy draft explains controller identity, purposes, rights, and contact route.

For US sites:

- Consumer request workflow covers access, deletion, correction, opt-out, and appeal where relevant.
- Sensitive personal information language is reviewed where applicable.
- Sale/share and targeted advertising indicators are reviewed.
- State-specific language is handled as a review packet, not an automatic legal guarantee.

## Operational Dashboard Checklist

Agency operators should monitor:

- Client sites with low scores.
- New tracker detections.
- Expiring or stale certificates.
- Open DSAR or consumer requests.
- Failed emails.
- Failed payment webhooks.
- Sites missing monthly proof packs.
- Sites with no recent scan.

## Client Handoff

Send each client:

- Installed platform and install date.
- Public policy links.
- Request form URL.
- Public certificate URL.
- First scan score.
- Open remediation items.
- Monthly proof pack schedule.
- Clear disclaimer that Zenvyra supports operational privacy workflows and does not provide legal advice.
