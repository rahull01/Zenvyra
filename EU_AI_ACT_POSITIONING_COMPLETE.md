## Positioning Narrative (Locked In)

**"Zenvyra is the EU AI Act readiness platform for AI-enabled businesses."**

Core workflow:
1. Inventory all AI systems (models, use cases, users)
2. Classify risk levels (prohibited, high-risk, limited-risk, general)
3. Generate required documentation (notices, risk assessments, disclosures)
4. Maintain compliance proof (audit trails, certificates, evidence)
5. Integrate privacy/cookie/consent underneath for complete trust proof

**Supporting ecosystem**: Website scanning, cookie compliance, policy generation, consent logging, and DSAR workflows—all connected to the AI readiness story.
